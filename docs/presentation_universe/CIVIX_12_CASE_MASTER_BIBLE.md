## NEO4J INVESTIGATION GRAPH CAPABILITIES
Every single entity, event, and piece of evidence detailed above is automatically projected into the **Neo4j Investigation Panel**. 
- Users will see nodes for `Person`, `Vehicle`, `Phone`, `Event`, `Property`, `BankAccount`, and `Organization`.
- Profile pictures (mugshots) are attached to the `Person` nodes as metadata pointing to the `Evidence Store`.
- You can right-click any `Event` node to open the **Timeline/Event Map** and physically trace the movement.
